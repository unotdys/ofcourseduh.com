Defying Entropy: Mike Johnson on the Group 42 CD, Anchor ID and AI Attribution

From a hacker CD‑ROM in the mid‑’90s to a UUID that could stop AI from forgetting who made what.

Mike goes back to the Group 42 CD, a 1994–95 archive built in HTML, and the “defy entropy” mantra it gave him. Then he explains Anchor ID, an open‑source way to tie your work and online identities to one UUID so it stays attributed to you when AI trains on it.
